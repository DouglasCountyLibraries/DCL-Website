Foundation Endowment

Funding the Future: Giving Now for Generations to Come

When you give to our endowment fund, you do more than support a library—you help secure a vibrant community hub for generations to come. You may designate a cash gift for the endowment today, or plan for future giving by reading further.

Your Support Makes a Lasting Difference
Our endowment fund, managed by the Rose Community Foundation, strengthens the library's ability to support and serve our community well into the future. Endowment funds provide financial stability that goes beyond annual taxpayer funding. Your legacy gift to the Douglas County Libraries Foundation Fund is tax-deductible.
A Gift That Keeps Growing
Your gift isn't just used once; it is invested so its impact lasts forever. The returns generate a steady, dependable stream of support year after year, allowing your kindness to continue giving far into the future.

Creating Extraordinary Experiences

While tax dollars cover daily essentials like maintaining buildings and keeping the lights on, your generosity makes the magic happen. You help fund early literacy programs, literacy scholarships, and free cultural programming that enrich lives every day.

A Safe Harbor in Changing Times

Economic climates shift, but your support ensures the library remains a reliable safe haven. An endowment acts as a comforting cushion, protecting beloved programs and resources even during lean financial years.
Your Personal Legacy of Learning
An endowment gift offers a meaningful way to honor your values or celebrate a loved one. It creates an enduring tribute that ensures your passion for literacy, curiosity, and learning lives on.
Thoughtful Financial Benefits
Supporting a 501(c)(3) foundation endowment through gifts of stock or legacy bequests can also offer valuable tax benefits, allowing you to maximize the impact of your generosity in a way that aligns with your family’s financial planning.

Frequently Asked Questions
  • What is an endowment? An endowment fund is money set aside (invested) to earn revenue to fund a charitable activity. Unlike a typical investment fund, the beneficiary of an endowment fund is the nonprofit organization. The principal value of the endowment fund is kept intact, while the investment earnings can be used. Thus, an endowment fund can be held permanently, allowing donors to support causes they care about in perpetuity. Excess income is reinvested in the endowment to grow the fund and protect it from inflationary pressures. Endowments provide perpetual support that strengthens the vitality of their nonprofit into the future.
  • Why does Douglas County Libraries Foundation have an endowment? Endowments help protect an organization against market volatility and unexpected expenses while also creating stability. A well-managed endowment sets an organization up for future success by laying a foundation for fiscal responsibility and taking pressure away from an annual fund to allow for more independence and freedom. Endowments are able to grow donations as investments and provide additional income for the nonprofit’s mission. When nonprofits set up an endowment, it not only opens another channel of income, but it can help the organization withstand economic downturns and unexpected expenses by managing resources well.
  • Who manages the library’s endowment? Local Denver nonprofit Rose Community Foundation manages Douglas County Libraries Foundation’s endowment, including administrative services and professionally managing the portfolio of funds. For more than 20 years, Rose Community Foundation has supported dozens of nonprofit organizations in creating and growing endowment funds.
  • May I donate to the endowment? A donation to an endowment lives on in perpetuity and continues to earn income for the Foundation. If you designate your donation for endowment, the Foundation will honor this designation. Donations to endowment funds are tax-deductible.
