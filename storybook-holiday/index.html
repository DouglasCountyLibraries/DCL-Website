A Storybook Holiday:

The
North Pole
EXPRESS

"We climbed mountains so high it seemed as if we would scrape the moon. But the Polar Express never slowed down."
— Chris Van Allsburg, The Polar Express
All aboard for holiday magic and wonder!
Join us for a fun-filled celebration of the beloved story "The Polar Express."
Enjoy family-friendly entertainment including live music and dance performances, delightful crafts, make-and-take cocoa gifts, festive photo ops, and a visit with Santa. Indulge in a delicious menu of food and drinks, oh-so-sweet treats, our signature hot cocoa bar, and local snack favorite Bob’s Roasted Nuts. Catch the holiday spirit here!
